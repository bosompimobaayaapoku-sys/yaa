Food

To be honest, I'm a foodie and I love to eat. But tell me, who on this planet doesn't love food? I mean, food is life! I love trying new things, and I'm always up for a challenge. I may not be the best cook, but I can definitely appreciate a good meal. Most of my favourite foods are from my home country, Ghana. I love traditional Ghanaian dishes like Jollof rice, Banku, Fufu, and Kelewele. But herh, the number one—the boss on my favourite food list—is ETO! I love ETO so much I could eat it every day. It's also easy to make: you use ripe and unripe plantains, peel them, boil them, and mash them with groundnut paste, my favourite fish (momoni), some salt, pepper, and groundnuts. Then you shape the mixture into balls and serve it with a boiled egg. So delicious!
